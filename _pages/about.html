About

I am a PhD candidate at HKUST (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Yi Cai and Prof. Jun Ma, and a joint-training researcher at the HKUST WorldMind Lab with Prof. Fangneng Zhan. I work on underwater robotics and embodied AI.

My research starts from one observation: underwater, a robot's actions decide the quality of what it can observe. Thrusters stir up sediment, motion blurs images, and currents push the robot off its viewpoint, so much of the data is degraded before anyone looks at it. I design control, estimation and planning methods that let a robot collect good data on purpose, and I validate them on real robots: about 440 real-robot trials so far, on an eight-thruster ROV and ground station I built.

Outside the lab I enjoy tennis, photography, travel and museums, and exploring the ocean.

Interests

Acquisition-aware control

Allocating thrust on redundant underwater robots so that the camera's view stays free of self-made disturbance, characterised with PIV.

Learning-based state estimation

Fusing learned visual keypoints and latent dynamics with model-based filters for fast, millimetre-level pose estimation.

Planning for observability

Current-aware path planning, multi-view 3D anchoring and vision-language skill selection for close-range inspection.

Research

T-ASE · under review

Acquisition-Aware Control Allocation

A PIV-calibrated thruster-wake objective for an eight-thruster ROV. Across 20 matched trial pairs, near-target flow fell by 49.6% and reconstruction RMSE by 34.9%.

RA-L · 2026

Millimetre-Accurate Underwater Pose Estimation

A keypoint network with latent-dynamics heads, fused with optical tracking in a tightly coupled EKF: 5.65 mm position RMSE at 62 FPS.

Ocean Engineering · 2026

Operator-Guided Coral Inspection

SAM2 masks triangulated across views into 3D anchors and turned into executable inspection viewpoints. Visual usability 82% vs 67% over 12 matched pairs.

RA-L · under review

CoralPlan: Vision-Language Skill Selection

The CoralVLM dataset and a LoRA-adapted Qwen2.5-VL-7B that chooses observation skills. Over 36 matched sim–hardware pairs: 77.8% observation completion, 63.9% joint success.

JOE · minor revision

Current-Aware Global–Local Planning

PSO global search coupled with local safety-corridor optimization, feeding local feasibility back into the global search for 3D navigation in ocean currents.

Platform

Ocean Isaac Sim & ROV Ground Station

An underwater simulation environment in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and a custom ground station with video, telemetry, trajectory control, logging and E-stop.
