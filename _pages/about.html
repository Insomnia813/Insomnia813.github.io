I build underwater robots that see better because of how they move. My research connects robot control, state estimation and vision-language planning so that a robot collects useful data on purpose, instead of leaving it to be filtered afterwards.

I am advised by Prof. Yi Cai and Prof. Jun Ma at HKUST (Guangzhou), and I am a joint-training researcher at the HKUST WorldMind Lab with Prof. Fangneng Zhan.

Research

Acting to observe

Underwater, a robot's own thrusters and motion stir up sediment, blur images and disturb fragile targets. Much of the data is degraded before anyone looks at it. I study how a robot can choose its actions so that what it observes stays useful, and validate every method on real robots.

Publications

Selected first-author work

Real-Time Millimeter-Accurate Underwater Pose Estimation via Tightly-Coupled Fusion of Vision and Optical Tracking

Yuer Gao, Tongqing Xu, Yi Cai

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), 11(2):1218–1225, 2026

A keypoint-based visual network with latent-dynamics heads, fused with optical tracking in a tightly coupled EKF: 5.65 mm position RMSE at 62 FPS.

PublishedPaper

A Vision-Based Operator-Guided Framework for Close-Range ROV Inspection of Coral Colonies via Multi-View 3D Anchoring

Yuer Gao, J. Luo, Yi Cai

Ocean Engineering, 2026

Pretrained SAM2 masks triangulated across views into 3D anchors and turned into executable inspection viewpoints, without per-scene retraining. Visual usability 82% vs 67% over 12 matched trial pairs.

PublishedPaper

Acquisition-Aware Control Allocation for Close-Range ROV Visual Inspection

Yuer Gao, Tongqing Xu, Q. Liu, Yi Cai

IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE)

A PIV-calibrated thruster-wake objective for redundant actuators. Across 20 matched trial pairs at practically equivalent robot motion, near-target flow fell by 49.6% and reconstruction RMSE by 34.9%.

Under review

CoralPlan: Observation Skill Selection and Execution for Underwater Robotic Inspection

Yuer Gao, Y. Zhao, Yi Cai

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)

The CoralVLM dataset and a LoRA-adapted Qwen2.5-VL-7B that selects observation skills, separated from execution by a planning/control interface. Over 36 matched simulation–hardware pairs: 77.8% observation completion, 63.9% joint success.

Under review

Bidirectionally Coupled Global–Local Path Planning for 3D Underwater Navigation in Ocean Currents

Yuer Gao, Yi Cai

IEEE Journal of Oceanic Engineering (JOE)

PSO global search coupled with local safety-corridor optimization, feeding local feasibility back into the global search; evaluated on a current-aware energy proxy, collision risk and planning cost.
